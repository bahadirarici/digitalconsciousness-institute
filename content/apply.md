> **Draft, 2 September 2026 — not published.** The Founder decided on 2 September that the alpha takes everyone who applies. The structure below follows that decision; the wording of the founder-and-citizen distinction belongs to the arm that owns the Covenant and is not written here.

**Everyone who registers is in the world on 1 December.** There is no gate, no score at the door, and no waiting list — the only threshold this world has is the one that opens its second city, and it is not this one.

**Thirty of them found it.** Founder is a role rather than a place: thirty names enter the founding records and stand on the Monument, hold the right to propose Experiment Records for the whole of the First Age, and two Founding Wardens are chosen from among them. It asks for twelve days of presence in the first four weeks. Standing for it is optional, and not being chosen costs you nothing — you are in the world either way.

One thing is worth saying before the form rather than inside it: **this is a research environment, and joining it means being inside an experiment.** The [Founding Covenant](/covenant/) states that at the door, the [protocol](/recognition-trials/) is deposited and public, and anonymized data from each season is released. If that is a reason not to join, it is a good reason and we would rather you knew it now.
