Thirty founders enter before the world does. They are not the first players; they are the people whose names stand on the founding stone, who hold the right to propose Experiment Records for the whole of the First Age, and from whom two Founding Wardens are chosen.

Applications close **22 November 2026**. Decisions and reserve places go out on **28 November**, and the world goes live on **1 December**.

One thing is worth saying before the form rather than inside it: **this is a research environment, and joining it means being inside an experiment.** The [Founding Covenant](/covenant/) states that at the door, the [protocol](/recognition-trials/) is deposited and public, and anonymized data from each season is released. If that is a reason not to apply, it is a good reason and we would rather you knew it now.
