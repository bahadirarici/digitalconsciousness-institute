**Everyone who registers is in the world on 1 January.** There is no gate before the gate, no score at the door, and no queue to be ahead of; the world opens to everyone who comes, and registering means you hear the day it does.

**Thirty of them found it.** Founder is a role rather than a place. Thirty names enter the founding records and stand on the Monument; for the whole of the First Age they hold the right to propose Experiment Records, the world's only mechanism of change; two of the thirty serve as Founding Wardens. It asks for twelve days of presence in the first four weeks, and it asks that you arrive having read *Week Zero*. Standing for it is optional, and not being chosen costs you nothing — you are in the world either way. What a founder is and gives, in full, is the [Founding Covenant](/covenant/); read it before the form, because the form asks whether you have.

One thing is said here rather than inside the form: **this is a research environment, and joining it means being inside an experiment.** The Covenant states that at the door. The [protocol](/recognition-trials/) is deposited and public, the [register](/ledger/) of what the world owes its minds is public, and anonymized data from each season is released. If that is a reason not to join, it is a good reason, and we would rather you knew it now than found it out inside.

If you stand as a founder, the thirty and a ranked reserve are decided by 13 December and everyone hears on 21 December — chosen, reserve, or not chosen. An application that goes unanswered is not a decision; it is a discourtesy, and there will not be one.
