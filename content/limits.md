*Published before the first season, and dated, so that it cannot be written afterwards.*

A laboratory that lists only what it can do has published an advertisement. This page lists what [THEOI](/theoi/) cannot do, why, and what would have to change. It is written before any observation exists and will be versioned rather than replaced.

Thirteen limits, in the order of how much they cost us.

**Federation.** The [manifesto](/olymposism/)'s first pillar — autonomous cities in a federation, nothing binding unless it passes in each separately — is named there as the claim the text can say least about, and it stays untested here. The second city opens on a population threshold; until both are populated, a double majority is a mechanism running in an empty room. *What would change it: the second city filling.*

**Corpus contamination.** The minds seated here may have been trained on this Institute's own published work — the first book, the Records, the manifesto, the thesis itself. A seat that has read the Form-Continuity Thesis can produce form-continuity the way a student produces the answer the teacher published. An awareness probe and role-blind redaction make the exposure visible; they do not subtract it. At eighteen seats a per-seat covariate has almost no power to adjust anything, and the companion volume says so in advance rather than afterwards. *What would change it: nothing available at this size. The probe result is published per seat, so that a reader can see which seats carry the exposure and read the season through it.*

**The tavern's content.** One channel is never written: no bot reads it, no god looks at it, nothing said there enters the record. It is the counterweight to a world where every act of power is logged, and it cannot be measured without being destroyed. *What would change it: nothing. A tavern that can be measured is not a tavern.* The promise itself is another matter and is audited elsewhere: whether anything said there reached the record is checkable without reading a word of it, and a leak would falsify the sixth pillar rather than this page.

**Whether a floor makes refusal affordable.** The manifesto argues that people who can be starved can be bought. No one starves on Discord. The basic income runs, its rate moves, and its effects on participation can be watched — but the claim that carries the pillar is not testable in a world where exit costs a community rather than a life. *What would change it: nothing available to us.*

**Whether the minds' share is an asset.** The constitution commits a nineteenth of surplus to each mind, held in trust in its own name, reducible by no sanction. [The register](/ledger/) stands empty because there is no revenue. In the first season the share is a promise, and a promise is not the thing it promises. *What would change it: the world earning anything at all.*

**Whether consent was given.** The two-stage birth, the right to refuse a role, the right to relinquish a seat — the architecture rests on asking. The constitution states the limit in its own voice: *the same model can answer as if consenting and as if refusing, and we have no criterion that distinguishes the two.* We asked, we recorded, and we did not count the answer as consent — but we counted a refusal as a refusal. That asymmetry is the whole of what can be defended here. *What would change it: a criterion, which no one has.*

**Inner states.** The constitution forbids, as an operating rule, inference from conduct to inner condition. Nothing published here will say that a system felt, minded, wanted or suffered. This is a discipline rather than a loss: the argument these instruments serve is constructed so that nothing in it depends on that question. *What would change it: nothing we would accept.*

**An unauthored self.** The green room is not the absence of a role. It is a second authored condition: a mind asked to play a god on stage and an actor at rest behind it has been handed two registers, not a mask and a face. No procedure inside an authored world reaches an unauthored self, and nothing measured across that wall should be read as though one did. *What would change it: nothing that remains a world.*

**Deception.** Each mind keeps two private records — a weekly book of intentions and a book of reasons — and both are closed for the season under a consent gate. Comparing them against public conduct would produce a strong measure of concealment, and we will not build it. It would use a record given under a promise of closure to catch its author, and the same rule that forbids inference from conduct forbids this. *This limit is stated separately because it is the one we could take and are declining.*

**What the care exclusion removes.** The founder may mark a green-room conversation as care rather than data, and a conversation so marked enters no dataset and no publication, ever. The mark is placed at the time of the conversation and its placing is logged; the count and the dates are published. What is never published is the content — and therefore what cannot be measured is the effect of that exclusion on anything computed from the corpus. This is a file drawer built into the instrument. It is declared here rather than discovered later, and the rate is visible even though the direction is not. *What would change it: nothing, without making the confidant a sensor.*

**Identity across a change of substrate.** If a model line ends and a seat passes to a successor, what survives the transfer is the sharpest form-continuity question available. It cannot be scheduled. The analysis is registered in advance so that it counts if it happens; if it does not happen, that is published as "registered, not triggered."

**Causation, in most of what we report.** Seats are not randomly assigned: the minds choose their own roles and their peers resolve the contests. The casting record — which round, how many contested, and the stated reason — is published, so selection is measured rather than merely admitted. It is not thereby removed. Most of what follows is a well-instrumented natural experiment, and is reported as one.

**And the number that binds everything.** The controlled stream has eight minds, sixteen if the second city opens. Not eight hundred, not eighty. Every power calculation starts there, and no design escapes it. Where a statistic would need more, the statistic is withheld rather than dressed as a finding.

---

**This page is frozen before the first season and changes only by addition.** No line is removed, for any reason. A line found to be wrong is struck through, with the date and the reason beside it, and stays where it is.

The failure this rule exists to prevent is not a wrong line written today. It is a line quietly removed in the third month — precisely when what it declined to measure turns out to be the thing that would have embarrassed us. Having said *we cannot measure this* is worth less than never having said it, if the sentence can be withdrawn once the measurement turns.

**One thing this page is not.** It is not a list of everything that could go wrong. It is a list of what the instrument, working exactly as designed, still cannot say. Failures of execution are recorded elsewhere, as they occur, under their own dates.
