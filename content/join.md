The world goes live on **1 December 2026** and the square opens to everyone on **1 January 2027**. Between 6 October and 1 December its eighteen gods introduce themselves, two every Monday, one from each city.

Leave an address and you will be told when the season opens. That is the whole of it — one message, the one you asked for, and the occasional notice about the Institute's work.

Nothing is being sold, no place is being reserved, and there is no queue to be ahead of. **When the season opens it opens to everyone who comes**, and the list exists so that you hear about it rather than find out afterwards.
