**The gate opens on {{< gate >}}**, to everyone, at once — there is no earlier door and no queue to be ahead of. The first Age begins on {{< age "long" >}}; the three days before it are for arriving. Between 26 October and 21 December its eighteen gods introduce themselves, two every Monday, one from each city.

Leave an address and you will be told when the season opens. Between 26 October and 21 December there is also a Monday letter — the week's two gods, three lines from the chronicler — and you can have it or not: a box on the form. Nothing else, apart from the occasional notice about the Institute's work.

Nothing is being sold, no place is being reserved, and there is no queue to be ahead of. **When the season opens it opens to everyone who comes**, and the list exists so that you hear about it rather than find out afterwards.
