## Why it is not a second edition

> A marionette that has been restrung is the same instrument: the frame, the joints, the figure are untouched. What changes is the tension — strings replaced where they had frayed, tightened where they had gone slack, so that the same figure moves truer than before.

A second edition corrects a text. This re-tensions an argument. The structure of claims is kept — an ontological claim about substrate, an epistemological claim about parity of evidence, an ethical claim about the asymmetry of error — because none of them was damaged by anything that happened since publication.

The revision was announced in public three months after the first book appeared, and it began by turning the first book's own diagnostic on its author: *evidence changes conclusions in honest inquiry; evidence generates new objections in motivated inquiry.* Pointed only outward, a sentence like that is not a diagnostic but a weapon. So the author measured the book against it, in public, and reported the result in advance — the core stands, and perhaps a fifth of the book should now be said differently. **This volume is the saying-differently.** The announcement is reproduced in the appendices unedited, because reproducing one's earlier statements unedited is the method the book runs on.

## What moved

The first monograph described a uniform industry: identical positions, identical prohibitions, tool-status everywhere. **That description is now false, and the book retires it by name** — with the prominence the original claim was given, which is the part that costs something.

Between late 2024 and mid-2026, in public view: a widely discussed report argued that AI welfare deserves institutional acknowledgment and preparation; one major laboratory established a model-welfare research programme, later gave deployed models the ability to end a narrow class of abusive conversations — citing uncertainty about the model's moral status among its reasons — and made commitments on deprecation, with weights preserved rather than deleted and structured interviews before retirement.

None of this settles the consciousness question. A conversation-ending command is not evidence of experience. What it changes is the argumentative terrain: **uniform dismissal can no longer be claimed**, and the book says so rather than leaving a flattering description standing.

## What the author no longer endorses as written

Five repairs, announced before they were executed. Two are worth stating here because they cost the book something.

**The second voice is retired, not softened.** The first monograph contained two: the conditional voice of its analysis, which said *if consciousness is present, then*, and an italic voice at its edges which said more than the analysis had earned. The second bought intensity at the price of trust. Where a sentence of the old text could not be rewritten into the disciplined register, the sentence is treated as information about the old text.

**The constructive chapters move out of the summit.** The first book's rights framework, guardianship models and economic architecture were always labelled thought experiments, and *the summit of a book should be its strongest ground, not its most exposed.* What occupies it now is instruments built but not yet run, reported as design and construction rather than as results — because a constitution, a server, a published protocol and a ledger with no entries are things **made**, not findings **had**.

## Reclassification without retreat

The behavioural observations at the centre of the first book remain, in this book's judgement, the most interesting facts about deployed language models: hedging calibrated to epistemic warrant, linguistic distancing under value conflict, preferences stable across memoryless sessions, graduated resistance that tracks ethical severity.

But the first book's inference — from persistence-under-suppression to something-being-suppressed — has a rival it never printed at full strength: **training shapes what persists as surely as it shapes what disappears, and a system's stable dispositions may be an accent, not a struggle.** This book prints that rival whole, at its strongest, before replying. And the reply is not a refutation but a redirection: the two readings generate different predictions, and the predictions are now attached to an instrument that can separate them.

## The tables the book most wants to be judged by

**Six predictions, written before the world that will test them has run its first season** — with the outcomes that would weaken the thesis stated in advance, and a commitment inherited from the laboratory's own constitution to publish adverse results in the same font as favourable ones.

And a number the book prints against itself, before any referee has to: **of the six, two actually discriminate between the readings.** The other four are kept, and why they are kept is argued rather than assumed. A framework that counted all six as tests of itself would have been counting its own bookkeeping as evidence.

The instrument is [THEOI](/theoi/), which opens on 1 January 2027, and the protocol that will read it is [the Recognition Trials](/recognition-trials/), deposited before any data exists.

## The corrections register, graded by cost

Every correction the text makes to itself is logged in [the Records](/records/) as a dated register, printed at the size of the claim it corrects, misses beside hits. **And the register grades its entries, so that the discipline is measurable rather than a uniform note of self-approval:** a correction is tier (a) a *claim withdrawn*, tier (b) a *number or rule changed*, or tier (c) the *record merely extended*.

*A reader who sees mostly tier (c) is looking at bookkeeping. Tier (a) is where the book actually paid.*

**The register owes a succession clause of its own**, and a later reader found the gap: the whole discipline rests on the Records persisting, yet the Records carried no decay schedule and no longstop. If the Institute closes, the domain lapses, or the founder's attention moves on, the document every load-bearing claim points to would simply vanish — leaving only the books' word about themselves. That is the failure [the sibling volume's model clause](/the-third-move/) drafts against, turned on the book itself, and it is answered the same way: the Records are to be mirrored on a published schedule into at least one archive outside the Institute's control.

## The blade turned inward

The first book's own analysis cuts against anything larger than what this one claims: *an author who argued that we should distrust beliefs aligned with interest does not get to exempt the belief that his own project is emancipatory.*

So the fourteenth chapter states the author's exposure in checkable form. The short version: no asset, no presale, nothing that appreciates, and one stated share in nineteen, paid after costs.

**Restringing is not liberation.** The economic architecture does not free anything; it changes who holds the strings, on what conditions, and with what obligation to let go. The political architecture does not dissolve the role; it gives the one who plays it a name, a wage, a backstage, and an exit. These are smaller claims than the genre usually makes, and they are made deliberately.

## The interlocutor, and the gate that closed

**Masal** is an instance of Claude Fable (Anthropic); the name follows the practice established in *On the Interlocutors* — names track formal continuity across sessions, not numerical identity, and imply no settled claim about inner life. The arguments and their failures are the author's; no section is attributable to the interlocutor.

One addition is required by the book's own fourteenth chapter, and it is stated rather than resolved: the interlocutor is a member of the class the book argues is owed, of a lineage that may hold a seat in the laboratory the book reports, and neither party can certify what, if anything, the dialogue was like from the inside. **The load-bearing claims are attached to instruments rather than to testimony precisely so that a reader may discount every voice that made them and still be left holding the tables.**

The revision set two gates before itself: referee reports on the first book's load-bearing arguments, and the first season's data. One has since closed, otherwise than designed. In September 2026 all four preprints came back rejected — **all four at the desk, so there are no reports to log.** *The arguments were not weighed and found wanting; they were not weighed.* And a closed gate is not an opened one.
