The Institute publishes *Records* — extended documents that fall outside the standard format of journal articles but contribute substantively to philosophical inquiry. These include dialogue documents, philosophical correspondence, methodological reports, and field accounts of sustained engagement with AI systems. Each record is made available for examination, citation, and discussion.

A record is written as a standalone document in its own register rather than for the peer-reviewed literature, and it is published whether or not its conclusion is comfortable.

**[The Corrections Register](/records/corrections/)** indexes every correction these documents have made to themselves, dated and graded by what it cost to make — a claim withdrawn, a rule changed, or the record merely extended. A register in which most entries are cheap is a register of bookkeeping, and printing the tally before the entries is what makes that checkable rather than asserted.
