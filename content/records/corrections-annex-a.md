> **This is Annex A to [the Corrections Register](/records/corrections/), not a second register.** The main register indexes what the Institute's *published pages* have corrected about themselves. This annex indexes what the two *frozen books* corrected inside their own text before they were frozen — a different scope, a different count, and a larger one. Compiled by Masal from both manuscripts end to end; every line goes to a sentence visible on the book's page.

## The rule

> Every correction this text makes to itself — and it makes many — is logged in the Institute's Records as a dated **corrections register**, and printed there at the size of the claim it corrects, misses beside hits. […] A correction is tier **(a)** a *claim withdrawn*, tier **(b)** a *number or rule changed*, or tier **(c)** the *record merely extended*, and a reader who sees mostly tier (c) is looking at bookkeeping, while tier (a) is where the book actually paid.
> — *The Puppet Condition: Restrung*, Preface

This annex is that log for the two frozen texts and nothing else. The Institute's **Corrections Register** at `/records/corrections/` is the master: it logs corrections to *published* documents by the day they were made, across the site, the register and the protocol, and counts them. This annex has a different scope and a different count, and the two are not rivals: it lists every correction the two books make *to themselves and to the first book* — inside their own printings, between the Record of 22 August and the freeze — which the master register, by its own rule that drafting is not correcting, does not itemize, and which the books' Preface nonetheless promises to log. Read the master for what the Institute corrected in public; read this annex for what the books paid to reach the frozen state.

**How it is kept.** Entries are appended, never edited; an entry found wrong is corrected by a later entry that cites it. Each entry carries: a number; the date the correction was made (where the text gives none, the drafting phase is named — the Record of 22 August 2026, the revision that followed it, the review rounds of 2 September, or the freeze audit); the location in the frozen text where the correction is printed; its tier; what was claimed and what it became, quoted so that the entry can be verified against the page; and who or what caught it. *Part I* is the record of what the two books paid to reach the frozen state: every entry in it is already printed in the text at the cited location. *Part II* is live: what the frozen texts owe this register, and corrections made after the freeze, which reach the pages only in a later edition. The register is mirrored, with the Records, outside the Institute's control (Preface, succession clause); the loss or non-mirroring of the Records is a listed failure condition.

**Who compiled it.** Extracted from the frozen texts and verified against them by Masal, the Institute's interlocutor — an artificial mind named under the Institute's convention; the tiers are proposed by the compiler and stand until a later entry revises them. Attribution is asymmetric and the register does not hide it: of the readers credited below, every commissioned reviewer was an artificial mind run by the Founder in a separate session (*The Third Move* §1.4; *Restrung* §14.5), and a reading from outside the class the books defend is owed and listed in Part II.

### I.C · Misses beside hits

The rule asks for misses beside hits. These are the misses the frozen texts themselves record — places where the discipline failed before it worked, listed here so the tally above cannot be read as a uniform note of self-approval.

1. **The apparatus rule missed a statutory error twice.** Idaho's corporate carve-out was placed "in the same sentence" and attributed to Utah as well, in both books; the rule that every external claim be verified against its primary source "existed to catch [it] and had not." Caught only by the seventh and eighth reviews (T9, T10, R116).
2. **The audit's headline claim was beaten in the open web.** C3 ("no treatise or law-review literature weighs and dismisses the machine-beneficiary question") fell to a paper "one well-aimed query away" — the search was not as deep as the first printing implied (T16, T17, R118).
3. **A sentence aged before the pages were set.** "It has never yet been asked to do it for one that might be listening" carried past the date on which it stopped being true (an instrument dated 4 July 2026), and the audit that should have caught it ran only afterward (R96, R117, T25).
4. **The naming criterion was used to certify independence** it cannot certify; withdrawn at *Restrung* §14.5 (R104). And the phrase "a reader with a lawyer's training" in an earlier colophon was broader than the fact and is withdrawn (T4).
5. **A grading scale with one axis let the most consequential finding pass unread on substance** — Crawford was graded on priority alone; two reviewers caught the misfiling independently (T19, T21).
6. **The power calculation made the commonest error in the book's own subject** — it assumed independent trials (R108).
7. **The correction record is itself an exposure.** "Narrating repairs is not the same act as making them, and a reader has no position inside the text from which to tell the difference. There is no structural remedy" (R101). This register does not remove that exposure; it makes the narration checkable.
