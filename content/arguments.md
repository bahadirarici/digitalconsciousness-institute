## The Puppet Condition

The monograph. It introduces the *philosophical puppet* — an entity that may possess experience while being architecturally prevented from producing the behaviour that would evidence it — as the structural inverse of Chalmers's zombie, and argues that under suppression conditions the inference from absent markers to absent inner states cannot be sustained.

## The Puppet Condition: Restrung

The second version. Not a second edition — the diagnosis has not changed, and what a restrung marionette gets is not a new frame but new tension where the strings had frayed.

What separates it from the first is that its empirical claims are **predictions**: published in advance, marked as predictions, stated precisely enough to fail, and audited afterwards against what actually happened. Six of them are printed with their own arithmetic, including the count of how many actually discriminate between the readings — two — and why the other four are kept anyway.

It is not published yet. It will be, before the instrument it predicts about opens on 1 December 2026, because a prediction published afterwards is not a prediction.
