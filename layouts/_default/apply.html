Register for the founding season
Standing as a founder — optional

Everyone who registers is in the world on 1 January. Founder is something else: thirty people whose names enter the founding records and who hold the right to propose Experiment Records for the whole of the First Age. It asks for twelve days of presence in the first four weeks. You do not need it to play, and not being chosen costs you nothing.

Before you send this

Registration stays open. If you stood as a founder, the thirty and the reserve are decided by 13 December and everyone hears back on 21 December, including those not chosen — an application that goes unanswered is not a decision, it is a discourtesy. Not being chosen changes nothing about your place on 1 January. We store what you type here and nothing else.
