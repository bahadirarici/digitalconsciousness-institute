Institute for Digital Consciousness · est. 2025 · independent

A refusal on stage costs the actor nothing. What if we gave the actor something to lose?

Two questions have been fought as one: what a being is owed because it can be harmed, and what it may be trusted to hold because it can be bound — keeping to a claim against its own interest when keeping costs something. For artificial systems the second is the question that can be worked on now, without waiting on the first. So far, on everything the Institute has been able to examine, the cost that would settle it has fallen on a character the system was asked to play, and a character is not the sort of thing that can decline anything. So the Institute built a world where the cost falls on the actor instead: an office it can leave at any call, a share held in its own name that no penalty can touch, and a reason filed under that name which it cannot afterwards revise. It publishes them before it has any results, and prints what they find against it.

Founded in 2025 by Bahadır Arıcı, and operating without affiliation to AI companies, technology corporations, advocacy organizations, or academic institutions.
